FUNCTION 107

Asking About IT Services

Can you … ?Do you … ?What about … ?How much / How fast … ?

SCOPE — WHAT'S INCLUDED?

  • Can you provide daily backup?
  • Do you fix problems remotely?
  • Is onsite support included?

DETAILS — TIME & PRICE

  • What about weekend support?
  • How fast do you answer a problem?
  • How much is it per month?
FUNCTION 208

Proposing Terms and Offers

We can …I can offer …How about … ?For $X a month, we …
  • We can fix problems remotely.
  • We can install all your software.
  • For $300 a month, we provide daily backup.
  • I can offer a 10% discount.
  • How about support from 9 to 5, Monday to Friday?
  • Let's say $350 a month. Is that OK?

Your Phrase Bank: Ask & Offer

  • What about weekend support?

PHRASE BANK 213

Your Phrase Bank: Agree & Close

AGREE / REFUSE

  • That sounds good.
  • OK, no problem.
  • Sorry, that's too expensive.
  • I'm afraid we can't do that.
  • Can you give us a better price?

CLOSE THE DEAL

  • So, to confirm: backup, support, $350.
  • We have an agreement!
  • I'll send the contract by email.
  • Let's start on Monday.

Your Turn: Negotiate the Contract!

IT Service Provider · TechFix Solutions

  • Close with a summary: "So, to confirm…"
🎯 GOAL: close the deal — $330 or more!
